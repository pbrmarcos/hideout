Shortcuts and settings

Everything you can do from the keyboard and every option in Settings, with animations that show how it works.

Keyboard shortcuts

Ctrl+Alt+V

Shows or hides Hideout from anywhere in Windows.

Ctrl+Alt+A

Email Password Sign in✓

In any login field, types username, Tab, password and Enter.

Ctrl+K

Google Analytics
Gmail

Opens search across logins, notes and agenda. Enter copies the password.

Ctrl+123

LoginsNotesAgenda

Switches between Logins, Notes and Agenda.

Ctrl+N/Esc

New: login

Ctrl+N creates a new item in the current tab. Esc closes the open panel.

The window

Starts at 30% of the screen width. The three buttons in the top bar snap it left, right or let it float. Dragging the bar releases it. The pin keeps Hideout above other windows.

Copying a password

Click the dots. The password goes to the clipboard flagged to stay out of Win+V history and cloud sync, and clears itself after the time you choose.

Agenda in plain language

Hideout understands dates written the way you say them (in Portuguese for now, as in the examples). With a date and time, the task becomes a Windows reminder.

    Settings

    Appearance

    ThemeSálvia, Lavanda, Areia, Névoa, Noite, Oceano, Rubi and Matrix. Each account keeps its own.

    Security

    Lock when idleFrom 1 minute to 1 hour. After that, only the PIN opens it.
    Lock with WindowsLock Windows (Win+L) or put the PC to sleep and the vault locks too.
    Clear copied password afterFrom 10 to 90 seconds. Only clears if the clipboard still holds the password.

    Window

    Start with WindowsStarts hidden in the tray, next to the clock, so reminders arrive on time.

    Features

    Auto-type loginTurns the Ctrl+Alt+A shortcut on or off. Hideout picks the login from the window title and asks when more than one matches.
    Encrypted attachmentsDrop PDFs or photos onto a login or note. Up to 10 MB each, locked with the vault.
    TemplatesLogin, card, Wi-Fi with QR code, ID document, bank account and server, each with the right fields.

    Account

    Change master password and PINA new password works on every computer. The PIN is per computer.
    New 2FA QR codeNew phone? Generate a new code for your authenticator.
    New recovery codeInvalidates the old one. Keep the new one away from the computer.
    Known computersRemove a PC and it will ask for username, password and 2FA again.

    Data

    Vault folderBy default, Hideout-Dados in your OneDrive. Each account has its own file.
    Backup and trashExport an encrypted copy any time. Deleted items stay in the trash for 30 days.
